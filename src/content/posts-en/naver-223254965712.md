A book that explains Nietzsche's philosophy in accessible terms

The first step toward finding value in life is acknowledging its emptiness

We should avoid being bound by the past and future, immerse ourselves in the present, stop comparing ourselves with others, and affirm our own strength

Instead of chasing immediate pleasure, we should work toward what we truly want

Suffering is something to overcome so that we can grow, rather than something that makes us despair of life

This book gave me a lot of comfort

> Regret adds another foolishness to foolishness
