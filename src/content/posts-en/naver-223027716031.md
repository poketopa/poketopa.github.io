The book's main emphasis is that “habits, too, have the magic of compound interest.”

You cannot change a habit overnight,

so you should add just a little to your usual routine and build new habits one percent at a time.

Try building a habit of drinking water when you wake up, then add making your bed after drinking water, then stretching, adding new habits little by little to those already established.

Just as multiplying 1 by itself 365 times still gives you 1, but doing the same with 1.01 gives you 37,

the idea is that expanding your habits little by little will eventually change your life itself.
