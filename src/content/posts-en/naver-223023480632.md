This was the first book I read at boot camp after joining the military, and it would hardly be an exaggeration to say it was my first novel in ten years

I think I picked it to read at boot camp because it was so famous

The story begins with three thieves entering an empty general store and answering letters that, strangely, keep arriving despite there being nobody there

I remember getting lost as I read because the novel was long and the narrative kept jumping between times and characters

I think I struggled a little because it was my first book in years

The stories of each character and the events at the orphanage were interesting, and the book left me feeling warm inside
