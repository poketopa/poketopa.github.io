I read this hoping to find an answer to "Why is my back like this?"

(Having read it, the only answer was to go back to crawling on all fours)

It is a book about the flaws in our bodies

Apparently, most of these flaws arose during human evolution

Biological evolution does not follow a blueprint like building a house; it adds onto what already exists, which apparently creates various defects

As you read, you discover so many flaws that you wonder how we manage to stay alive at all

I enjoy gaining new scientific knowledge, so I had fun reading this
