There are countless people in the world, and objectively, I am not particularly special among them

In any field, someone is bound to be better than me, and just as I take no special interest in others, I am probably just another person to them

In The Little Prince, the fox talks about being tamed

Being tamed and becoming friends means becoming special to each other among countless other people, recognizing each other's footsteps, and getting excited before you even meet

It also means thinking of your friend when you see the color of a wheat field

And feeling as though all the stars in the sky are greeting you

I think what ultimately keeps us going through life is the people who consider us special, and the feelings that make them special to us

Of course, self-improvement matters too, but as the line "What is essential is invisible to the eye" suggests, I also thought I should avoid becoming like the adults on the planets in the novel, obsessed with money and power
