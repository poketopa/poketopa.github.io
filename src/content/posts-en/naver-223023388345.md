I read this shortly after arriving at my military unit.

It was so famous and widely discussed that I decided to try it.

The story and vocabulary were accessible, and it was easy to read, so I flew through it.

I remember how difficult customers change through the protagonist's words and actions.

Everyone has wounds or worries that can sometimes come out in the wrong ways, but I thought a single kind word might be able to soften those wounds.
