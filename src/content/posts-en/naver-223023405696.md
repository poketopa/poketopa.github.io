The title comes from “Do you like Brahms…” in a letter a character sends in the novel; apparently, the point is that it ends in three dots rather than a question mark.

I read it on a senior soldier's recommendation.

Honestly, it was a somewhat dizzying book.

It portrays, from the woman's perspective, the psychology of a relationship between a cheating man and a woman who cannot give him up.

The male protagonist loves the female protagonist, but cannot give up the thrill of relationships with other women,

while the female protagonist hates him and loves him, blaming herself for it while being shaken by his every action.

Her emotions swing back and forth, but they were feelings I had experienced at least once in life, so I could relate as I read.
