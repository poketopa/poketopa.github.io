I read this at military boot camp

I was not used to reading books, so reading itself felt very strange

A boy on the cover and a title like Almond—I had no idea what it would be about

It has been a while since I read it, so I do not remember the exact details

It described events that happen to a boy who cannot feel certain emotions because part of his brain does not function, and the thoughts and feelings he has along the way

The fresh premise and exciting plot made it fun, and it was easy to read, so I finished it quickly
