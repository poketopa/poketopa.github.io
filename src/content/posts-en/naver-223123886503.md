> People who look impressive but have no substance lose opportunities?
>
> If you do not look impressive, you will never even get an opportunity to lose.

Was it because I wanted to drive a Porsche too?

Those lines drew me into reading the book

Because I thought they were persuasive to some extent

The position makes the person.

You become what you think.

Fake it till you make it.

I agree with sayings like these, that a sense of responsibility toward the situation you are in helps you grow, so I thought the book might give me a major insight, but...

There was not really anything that met my expectations

The author is a home shopping host, and most of the book used psychology to explain how to sell products to customers effectively

The topic was quite interesting, but the content did not leave much of an impression

A disappointing book.
