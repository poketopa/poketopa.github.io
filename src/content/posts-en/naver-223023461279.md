## The Da Vinci Code

---

My dad recommended this book

When I first brought it home and opened it, I closed it straight away

It was the first time I realized books had difficulty levels

Even the characters' names were hard, and the descriptions were on another level

After training myself a little(?) with other books, I could read it when I opened it again

It is a novel set in the Louvre, beginning with the death of a museum curator

The story follows the solving of riddles to uncover the secrets of Christianity, discover the truth, and find its product, the Holy Grail

The descriptions were sophisticated enough that I had to reread some passages, but the exciting progression of events and solid worldbuilding drew me in

Especially when it discussed truths concealed by Christianity and the evidence for them, I read without even realizing it was fiction

I would like to read the author's earlier book, Angels & Demons, too
