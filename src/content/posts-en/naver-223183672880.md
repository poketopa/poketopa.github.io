Like No Longer Human and Demian, most of it consists of their monologues. I kept alternating between relating to their thoughts and recoiling from their increasingly extreme actions

There were many sentences and descriptions of inner feelings that I liked,

And it made me think that having a reason to live greater than myself could lead to more frightening consequences than I had imagined
