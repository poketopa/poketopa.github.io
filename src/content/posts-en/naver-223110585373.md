Science fiction short stories.

I found the setting fascinating: while creating a new humanity, a scientist tries to abandon a child resembling herself (a failed creation), but feels as though she is denying her own existence, so she stops her research and builds a new village. Because it is a short story, though, we never learn what happens afterward, which was a little disappointing.

Learning the meaning of the line “If we cannot go at the speed of light,” which gives the book its title, also stayed with me. I found the perspective on the difference between dying on Earth and dying in space interesting.

When I read novels, something about human relationships and feelings resonates with me, and I think that makes me feel a great deal.
