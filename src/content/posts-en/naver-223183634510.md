A book about how to live, based on human instincts and psychology.

Its basic premise is that life is suffering.

It tells us what we need to know and how to act to endure our brief lives.

I learned many things: that hierarchy is rooted in the deepest biological instincts, making how we appear to others far more important than we might think; that in conflicts with loved ones, there are times to confront the issue rather than always give in; and that reaching the bottom of our emotions can be a way to resolve a problem.

What made the book uncomfortable to read was its strong Christian bent and excessive discussion of the Bible. (I don't know the Bible, so I had no clue what he was saying.)

The idea that humans are far stronger than we think as long as our spirit does not break resonated with me. Let's keep going.
