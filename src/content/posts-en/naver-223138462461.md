I borrowed this from a career soldier in our office.

I remembered briefly reading it when it was serialized as a webtoon,

and only knew it had a reputation as a masterpiece.

I sobbed reading it.

Rather than teaching some grand lesson,

it reminded me of the preciousness of everyday life and gave me another chance to think about death.
