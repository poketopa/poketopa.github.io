The book has just one message

Leverage everything in your life.

Here, leverage means maximizing the time you spend developing and growing your abilities while minimizing simple labor that anyone can do

Put simply, read a book in a taxi instead of driving yourself..

It means using your time efficiently to raise your market value as much as possible

The idea feels fresh, but I think there are certainly areas where you inevitably have to be leveraged yourself until you have built financially stable wealth

Still, I think the concept of leverage is essential to success in life
