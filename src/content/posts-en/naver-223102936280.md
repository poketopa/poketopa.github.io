Do you remember what you had for lunch a month ago?

How about a week ago?

How about yesterday?

We do so many things every day

Yet cannot remember them all and let them slip away

If we could remember just a tenth of what we see, experience, and learn in life, we could live much better lives

To keep that knowledge from simply slipping away, we need to build a habit of recording things

Writing helps not only with knowledge but also with our own emotions

Tracing our feelings back to their source in our minds can bring us closer to solving problems quickly

My circumstances are not ideal yet, and the habit is not firmly established, but I am building a habit of writing. The most important thing is consistency!!
