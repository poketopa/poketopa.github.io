"I don't discriminate"

Everyone says this

Then who on earth is doing the discriminating, and who is being discriminated against?

"I respect them, but I would rather they weren't around me"

Though I think it may be inevitable

It is something I really hate hearing

"Women are bad drivers and men are not attentive to detail"

I call it prejudice, but I agree to some extent myself

Should a transgender person use the men's or women's restroom?

Should citizens affected by disability rights protests simply accept it as unavoidable and put up with it?

Controversies over discrimination never stop

No, they cannot stop

Many of these problems are impossible to resolve

In this world where everyone discriminates and is discriminated against

What we need to do

Is simply watch what we say
