## Dallergut Dream Department Store

---

I read it because a discharged soldier had left it in a drawer.

Honestly, neither the cover nor the title appealed to me, so I wasn't curious.

It was more entertaining than expected, and I finished it quickly.

Maybe books that stimulate my imagination like this suit me more than I realize.

The story about the married couple in volume two nearly had me bawling.

I've been dreaming a lot since coming to the military, too.

Am I buying out the whole department store?
