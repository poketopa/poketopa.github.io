A couple moves into a house where a murder happened, their neighbors, and a detective who comes to investigate the murder

The protagonist tries to identify the real culprit behind the past crime

It was fun trying to solve the mystery among so many characters

I read closely to keep track of the relationships between the protagonist and the other characters, and the gripping case drew me in too

Maybe because I have not read many mystery novels, when the culprit was revealed, I felt like I could not have deduced it even by carefully reading what came before

I guess I am still a total scrub
