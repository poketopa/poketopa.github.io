Dictatorship or elitism might, in some ways, be a more ideal system than democracy.

But the decisive reason dictatorship or elitism struggles to succeed

is the possibility of corruption.

Add an ignorant public that never questions the system, and corruption accelerates.

To live with dignity,

to protect ourselves,

we must keep learning and stay vigilant.

Because knowledge is power.

“In the end, it was impossible to tell who was a pig and who was a human.”
