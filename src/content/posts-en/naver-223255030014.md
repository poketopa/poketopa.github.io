A book about the harmful effects of smartphones and social media

People always carry their phones

They become very anxious without them

I had never realized that I had never once seen an exception to this in my life

How many benefits could we gain by putting down the phones we check hundreds or thousands of times a day?

How much could we grow?

---

Humans release dopamine when they encounter new people or information, and adding randomness maximizes that release

Social media, Reels, and Shorts were made by exploiting this to the extreme

That makes them extremely addictive and harmful

(The claim that Reels and Shorts work much like slot machines gave me chills)

Even if you turn your phone face down or silence it, the very effort of trying not to look at it consumes energy in your brain and reduces your concentration

You have to put it somewhere else entirely to prevent this

(Even that is not perfect. Once you have used a phone, escaping withdrawal symptoms is nearly impossible)

The more you use your brain, and the harder you try to use it, the better it functions

People who simply look at artworks remember them longer than people who photograph them, and people who find their own way develop their hippocampus more than those who use navigation

Exercise has a very positive effect on concentration and brain function. Running is particularly effective
