A husband who earned wages all his life and a wife who turned his earnings into substantial wealth.

These are a father's stories about money for his son.

Reading books about economics, I found that the broad framework is the same, but the smaller elements differ.

Son, You Need to Study Money seemed to place particular emphasis on the habit of accumulating money and the habit of keeping it.

It says to build seed money by thinking carefully and decisively cutting out things you do not need, and to grow your wealth by caring for the money and possessions you use.

I liked the writing in the form of letters from a father to his son, and thought its practical lessons were helpful to me.
