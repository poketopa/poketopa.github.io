A book about habits and tips for saving money in everyday life

Something felt off from the beginning

Use the cheapest phone plan to save money and spend less time on your phone—two birds with one stone~

That made me think, hmm...

Then came

"Go to an apprentice hairdresser and you can get a haircut for free~" I closed the book when I saw that

I cannot call it a bad book, and it certainly contains everyday wisdom and useful tips

But it did not fit my values at all

I want to earn money to live better, not ruin my quality of life to save small amounts

Of course, I do think some restraint is essential for building seed money
