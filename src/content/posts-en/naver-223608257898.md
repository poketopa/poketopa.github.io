I think people are at their most irrational when they are in love

Perhaps those seemingly inhuman moments are, paradoxically, when we are most human

The Sorrows of Young Werther vividly portrays love's effects on people and the irrational thoughts and actions it brings.

Watching Werther fall into self-loathing and wander without rational thought as he loves Lotte shows just how far love can push a person

He looks foolish and stupid, but I doubt many people could simply condemn him for it.

Love is, in itself, an upheaval of emotion, and sometimes those emotions go beyond the limits of reason

I felt that Werther's actions and thoughts were no different from those of people today.

Even though it is a classic novel, I could easily relate to his thoughts

Acting on instinct and emotion rather than rational judgment, and feeling joy and anguish, seem to be timeless, unchanging qualities of love

And the whole time I was reading, I kept thinking of Minseok

It was The Sorrows of Young Minseok

> Human beings are all the same. Most spend most of their time earning a living. The little free time left to them becomes a source of anxiety, and they use every means available to escape it.
