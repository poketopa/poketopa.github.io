I think politics should mean dedicating yourself to your country and working to make it better.

Even when I questioned a policy, I assumed there must be something I did not know or understand.

I thought I could not casually judge and criticize decisions made after careful deliberation by the people representing our country.

But this book discusses misguided policies implemented in the past and present.

What shocked me most was the Biden administration's debt ceiling negotiations in the United States.

I could not possibly see a policy that amounted to passing a ticking time bomb around, and a maliciously chosen period for suspending the ceiling, as rational.

I think I had failed to grasp the essence of politics.

If perfect policies could be implemented, would political parties need to exist?

Policies can serve a particular party, its supporters, a social class, or even an individual. Perhaps everyone speaks up to benefit themselves.

A policy that is terrible for me may be wonderful for someone else, and vice versa.

Perhaps what is truly terrible is cynicism and skepticism toward politics, rather than the policies themselves.
