In fifteen years, please kill the guys who murdered my daughter.

In fifteen minutes, please report the teammates who murdered my mom.

This book was advertised everywhere.

I didn't actually know it was the book from those ads when I started reading, but soon recognized it from the story.

It was my first mystery novel, and I lost track of time reading it.

Other people called it “predictable,” but I enjoyed it—maybe because I haven't read many books.

I want someone to give me money on a bridge, too.
