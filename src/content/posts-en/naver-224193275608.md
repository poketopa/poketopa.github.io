> Homo ludens means “the playing human.”
>
> It is a view of humanity that understands play as our essence.

We usually treat running around as play and think of quiet activities such as studying as something other than play,

but most human activities can be said to take the form of play in that we establish rules and roles and act within them.

What is surprising is that imposing constraints on ourselves through play can actually give us true freedom.

I think this connects to existentialism. Perhaps humans, born without any purpose or value, created social rules in the form of play to produce a sense of achievement and meaning.

I think the moral freedom we feel when acting according to our conscience within human-made laws, truths, and justice is one such meaning.
