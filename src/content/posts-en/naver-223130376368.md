Just enough happiness to make me happy

That is all the happiness I need

A book about how to find happiness in everyday life,

Whom to keep close and whom to keep at a distance

To live a happy life

> Some children cannot go to a bathhouse because they are embarrassed by how much dead skin comes off.
>
> Others cannot start working out at a gym because they think their bodies look too unimpressive.
>
> We should not project our perfectionism onto ourselves.
>
> We may end up never going to the bathhouse or gym, drifting further from happiness
