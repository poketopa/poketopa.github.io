### Choices are vague, the process is anxious, and the results always leave something to be desired

I know. As long as I keep trying, I will keep wandering, and these feelings will follow one another until I die.

Still, I have not grown used to them.

I did not even write retrospectives for Levels 1 and 2, despite everything that happened.

But I do not want to blame myself.

Failing to understand myself has always led only to self-blame, and I want to believe I was never lazy.

For the first time in my life, I answer yes when asked whether I worked hard.

---

### A life of giving everything

`Burnout.` A word I have heard countless times in my life.

Whenever I heard it, I could not relate to how it felt.

Because when I looked back and asked whether I had ever burned through everything I had, my answer was absolutely not.

Partly it may have been my lack of ability, but partly it was because giving my all seemed to imply that the point I reached was my limit.

#### But

At Woowacourse, I am clearly immersed.

I wake before 6 a.m., reach campus at 6:30, leave at 7 p.m., swim and work out, then go home and sleep.

In this routine, any leisure time after I lie down eats into my seven hours of sleep, so I do not even want to spend time on that kind of entertainment.

I used to look at people who maintained what everyone calls an exemplary, productive routine and wonder what enjoyment they got out of living like that. Now that life is mine.

So do I find this life hard?

Of course it is hard.

My head is full of concerns and worries all day, and I clash with people.

Still, the feeling that I am living each day well gets me through today.

---
### Rising standards

I have grown enormously at Woowacourse.

Technically and personally, every day brings growth and new experiences.

I could feel it myself, so even giving everything every day felt neither wasteful nor especially exhausting.

On the other hand, as both my level and my standards rose each day, what I had done before began to seem insignificant.

Even the portfolio and résumé I thought were better than those around me felt like `nothing special`.

Probably because I keep holding them to new standards every day.

From talks by industry professionals and feedback from coaches, the other crew members and I were always raising our standards.

Even what I had built so far felt like `something everyone can already do`, like a book on AI methodologies that is already outdated a month after publication.

I had to keep burning and aim for something bigger.

I thought I must never let the fuse leading to my dream go out.

That makes things a little hard for me.

---
### Fear

After a team-building process full of talk and trouble, I joined a team I was happy with.

But fear of new people and a new assignment blossoms again.

Now that the Level 3 team project has started, fear, a little anticipation, and uncertainty about finding a job leave me feeling strange again.

It was the same helpless uncertainty I felt when Level 1 began and I tried to get close to people, when pair programming revealed the skill gap between us, and when I felt that gap would never close.

I had always considered myself optimistic, so I wondered why I was so stressed.

Because these were situations the old me would never have worried about.

I do not begrudge investing today and giving everything to create the future I want. But, on the other hand, I seem to have become deeply afraid of things that point to an unsettled present and an uncertain future.

---
### A project idea shaken up

Our team could not build a service during Level 3.

Because our coach's feedback was that the service's very direction was wrong and the topic was not a good one.

We had to rebuild from the initial planning stage, and the process brought many hardships.

Still, after a full month of thinking, meeting, and arguing together, we came up with a new plan.

There are no visible results at all, but I feel our team has become as solid as it was shaken.

I definitely feel better too. Probably because I feel the fuse has been lit again.

I still do not know whether these emotional swings are positive for me or harmful.

For now, I simply hope to grow once more through the project.

Even if I cannot fully overcome these feelings, I believe I can eventually get used to them and make it through.

---

### People

What lets me make it through each day at Woowacourse? Without question, the people.

In fact, looking back even a little at past events showed me how much I love the people around me.

Near the end of Level 1, I felt really bad when I heard that our villages would change.

Because I thought that before closeness passes a certain threshold, physical distance becomes emotional distance.

By the end of Level 2, those bonds had passed that threshold, so I thought we would be fine whatever happened and did not worry.

When the Level 3 project started, our seating changed.

So that everyone could sit with their project teams.

Unexpectedly, I found it quite hard.

Being separated from the people I was always with was almost cruelly painful to me.

We were still in the same building and could see each other every day, but I was surprised to feel this way over such a slight distance, and realized how much I relied on them.

Even now, several times a day, a feeling of emptiness suddenly comes over me. But it comes purely from wanting them beside me, rather than from fear that we will drift apart, and that proves how deeply I care for them.

Countless people have passed through my life, but I wonder whether I have ever missed anyone this much. One day they may become people who belonged to a particular season of my life, but right now, they are the people I most want to hold on to.

I am always grateful.
