## Viktor Frankl's Man's Search for Meaning

---

A book reflecting on the meaning and value of human life, written after surviving a German concentration camp

Even in extreme conditions, as long as people have a reason to live, they can stay present and look forward to the future

That reason differs according to each person's beliefs, but as long as it exists, people do not collapse

Life is a succession of suffering, but every suffering holds lessons and opportunities, and recognizing that fact is itself an attitude toward life and a form of wisdom

Even someone in a harsh environment can choose for themselves what kind of person they will become

Circumstances only tilt that choice; ultimately, who I am now is the result of my own choices, and I can change who I become through my choices too

---

This book gave me a lot of comfort

I deeply related to the idea that people inevitably grow through suffering

It was something I had been feeling painfully clearly lately

Living in the unfamiliar environment of the military, and being physically unwell on top of that, felt hopeless and brought me deep depression, but I also discovered and learned a great deal through that suffering

Reading about the author's experiences in such extreme conditions also made my own situation feel like almost nothing by comparison

If suffering is something I experience relatively like this, I thought that when facing future hardships, it would be better to think about what I can learn and how to overcome the situation, rather than sink into depression and self-blame
