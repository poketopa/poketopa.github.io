Packed full of successful people's habits, routines, values, and thoughts

What I took away from the book

1. There is enormous variety in the paths to success and in successful people's thinking and values

2. Reading, writing, and meditation matter

Whatever thoughts or habits I have, I can succeed if I am highly capable

But the more good habits I have, the better my chances of success

Reading and keeping records to expand the inspiration and memories of everyday life, and meditating to organize my thoughts and focus my mind, are essential

Let me organize the titans' habits and try applying them to my own life
