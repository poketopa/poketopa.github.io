A book about the history, examples, and current state of inflation

While studying economics, I had more or less thought inflation meant economic growth, but the book says it is more dangerous and harder to manage than I imagined

It discusses the Phillips curve

The idea is that when inflation lowers the value of money, companies hire more workers, so unemployment also falls

But a premise of this Phillips curve is that workers must not realize that inflation is reducing the value of their labor

I thought the workers in this account were foolish, but looking around me, I felt that few people seemed to attach much significance to rising prices or think about the depreciation of their own labor

Since the economy is one of the most important aspects of life, I should study harder and make more effort so that no one takes my livelihood away
