We assume our feelings will follow our thoughts

We feel especially certain of this when we have to uphold a moral principle

How many people can keep their promise to love and stay beside a friend, partner, or family member, whatever they become?

How many people can honestly say they would not be able to stay?

The protagonist of The Metamorphosis made me think of elderly people.

I wondered whether an elderly person with dementia might be no different from the protagonist who has turned into an insect

If I had to choose whether the body or the soul defines a person's humanity, I would choose the soul

I think remembering their relationship with me defines that person and makes them feel meaningful to me

In that sense, dementia felt like such a terrible curse

Losing memories of close relationships and leaving only a body and faint recollections

To me, it felt like death, perhaps even something beyond it

Because it makes people despair and loathe themselves by letting them realize, very slowly, a little at a time, that thoughts and feelings are never the same

Can I become someone whose very existence is a reason to be loved?
