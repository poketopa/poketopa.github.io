An unmotivated, bored protagonist

Thoughts and feelings that seem no different from a sociopath's, yet that anyone might have had

Ultimately, he was me beneath the mask of social norms

The characters who fixated on morality rather than the essence of feelings and events seemed no different from real life

And every word of the final scene, where he pours out his true feelings before the priest, left an impression

In a world where everyone wears a mask, the bare-faced protagonist was a stranger
