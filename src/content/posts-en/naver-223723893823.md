I was disappointed by how much of it concerned minor details.

To recap what I remember

1, We live in an age unconstrained by age or roles.

Older people can take on challenges, and younger people can help older people or vice versa. (The emergence of new content)

2. We live in an age when preserving what we have takes priority over taking chances.

Because of the unstable economy and social atmosphere, people prefer to keep what they have and make small, certain gains rather than risk their money, time, and other resources on a challenge. (Among younger generations)

3. The economy is in really serious trouble.

Things were already terrible in 2024, but apparently 2025 will at least be no better than things are now.

They say the harder things get for a country, the more trivial things appear in the news. This book had so many tiny, trivial topics that I could not concentrate.

(Decorating pimples was just ridiculous)

Is the situation really that difficult, with so little to be happy about in Korean society?

It feels a little bleak, but I think times like these are exactly when I need to increase my own value.

In a boom, build your assets; in a downturn, build your own value...
