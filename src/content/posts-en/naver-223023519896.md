A fellow soldier recommended this along with A Thousand Blues.

It is a collection of short stories.

The author's remark, “Some things in this world cannot be restored, however much time passes or whatever means we use,” hits painfully hard.

The stories are tragic and hopeless, but every one of them is so absorbing that I could not look away.

This was the most engrossing book I have read, and it lingered with me long afterward.

The story “Looking for a Child,” in particular, stayed with me.

I want to read Kim Young-ha's other books, too.
