Judgment after death.

In religion, people commonly think karma and sins are settled after death, but the laws of the afterlife differ from those of this world.

None of that here.

What would I want to be born as in my next life?

What disadvantages would I choose,

and what strengths?

What did my past self choose, I wonder?

What are my disadvantages, and what are my strengths?
