A novel whose characters possess the utter ordinariness and humanity I want to escape. At first I found them unpleasant, then I empathized with them, and finally I felt sympathy for them.

They treat other people's feelings lightly while regarding their own love as priceless

Hurt the people they love out of needless spite

And cannot even understand their own fickle hearts. Those qualities made it impossible to hate them.

As the saying goes, hate the sin but not the sinner. Perhaps there are only bad situations, and hating a person themselves is never easy.

Books like this always leave me with a heavy heart.

I wonder whether I have ever felt such a radiant moment, or whether there will be a moment in my life when I feel those emotions.

Perhaps human beings envy not only possessions and status, but even other people's feelings.
