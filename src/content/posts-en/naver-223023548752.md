This was the first book about economics I read.

It felt like a textbook on the basic framework of economics and the patterns that repeat themselves.

It calmed me down when I was all greed and ambition.

I read it quite a while ago, but one passage stuck in my mind.

“When we see someone surrounded by luxuries such as fine clothes and a nice car, the one thing we know for certain is that they have that much less money.”
