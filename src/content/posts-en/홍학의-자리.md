An absorbing novel with multidimensional characters.

It laid bare human frailty in extreme circumstances, making it hard to simply condemn their choices, and I was curious about how the story would unfold.

I felt a lot of sympathy for Dahyun. Dahyun was hated by others for their family circumstances, their parents' choices, and their sexual orientation, yet none of these were choices Dahyun had made. That felt contradictory.
