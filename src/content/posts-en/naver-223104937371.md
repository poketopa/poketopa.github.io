I like reading books about psychology

Because they help me understand why I feel the emotions I experience in everyday life

We feel inferior when someone close to us succeeds, and superior when they fail

Our minds, selfish as they may seem, are simply programmed that way

We sometimes feel guilty about this, but we have to accept that we cannot help it

This book helped me through that process, so I really enjoyed reading it

The thought I had as I closed the book was that all human psychology

Comes from "the belief that I have to be the best"

When people think they are not the best, they deny it through all sorts of negative thoughts

Even though learning from someone better than us would actually be better for us

We have to recognize and overcome these kludges in our own minds
