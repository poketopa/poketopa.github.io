In the evening and the early hours, we usually eat dinner, relax, and spend time on social media, games, or YouTube.

When I stay up late, that time stretches out, and I think I feel more self-disgust and regret than enjoyment.

The miracle morning means moving those wasted evening hours to the morning and using them productively.

After watching YouTube, I decided to try the miracle morning, and read the book to see whether it offered any special know-how.

There wasn't much advice that was particularly useful, but it was full of energetic encouragement. I came away with a pep talk.
