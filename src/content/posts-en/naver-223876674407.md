The author's flippant language, the subject matter, and the characters were unpleasant

But above all, the loss of meaning in the lives of middle-aged men and women, described in a calm tone that seemed resigned to that state, was chillingly disturbing.

I am afraid I might live a life no different from theirs, like an ember quietly dying out.
