Find “the one thing that will make everything else unnecessary” in achieving your goal, and focus on it.

Avoid multitasking, block distractions other than that “one thing,” and devote yourself to it alone.

People who think big and dream big can achieve great success.

Those who cannot even imagine great success can never succeed.

Write down your dream somewhere you can see it,

and surround yourself with people who can support your dream, think alongside you, and coach you.

The book uses the phrase “focusing.”

It is about how to choose the “one thing” you need.

Reading it made me wonder what my own one thing is.

I should take some time to think about it.
