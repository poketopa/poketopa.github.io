Where the economics books I had read focused on "How can I become rich?"

This one explained "How does the global economy work, and what can we make use of within it?"

It was a real help to someone like me who knew little about the real-world economy

I learned about economic cycles and history around the world, particularly in the US and China: inflation and deflation, politics and trade, quantitative easing and tapering, and more

I especially liked the explanations of the advantages, disadvantages, and cautions for different investments

Looking at economics through the broad frame of the world rather than the narrow frame of "me" was quite different and interesting

As I gradually learned the reasons behind familiar things I had never understood, I grew curious about other things and wanted to study them too

A very helpful book

The cute illustrations are a bonus
