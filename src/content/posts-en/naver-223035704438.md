George Orwell's dystopian novel 1984

A state that worships a fictional figure, fabricates the truth, and distorts and erases history

Citizens who are thoroughly watched and brainwashed around the clock

Yet everyone takes it for granted and cannot even think of resisting

In these circumstances, the protagonist questions reality and tries to stand against those in power, but ultimately conforms to that reality

This was my first dystopian novel

The world was quite fascinating, and I enjoyed the beginning

It did feel a little slow because the book was long relative to its plot

I was struck by how the protagonist's complete brainwashing at the end was expressed as "a bullet passed through his brain"
