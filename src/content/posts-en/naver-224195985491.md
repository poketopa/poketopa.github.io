Thanks to neuroplasticity, the more we use our brains, the more they develop.

Reading, writing, speaking, and communicating especially expand our thinking and abilities.

Yet people these days tend to outsource their brains.

They avoid thinking for themselves and chase only short bursts of pleasure.

If we rely on AI for too much, we will lose the ability to critically examine everyday problems,

And if we cannot escape the swamp of short-form content, we will lose the joys of everyday life one by one.

As the saying goes, it is not that we have no time to read; we have no time because we do not read

Attempts to outsource our brains in pursuit of convenience and entertainment will ultimately rob us of the ability to think and the happiness of life.
