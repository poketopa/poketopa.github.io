This is a difficult novel to understand without knowing the atmosphere of its era.

I could not grasp the distinction between the nouveau riche and old money that the protagonist feels inferior about and tries to overcome, and the underlying emptiness and the characters' dryness made it hard to become invested in anything.

Works that cannot be fully enjoyed without prior knowledge always leave me a little disappointed.
