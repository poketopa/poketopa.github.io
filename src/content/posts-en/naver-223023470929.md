I bought this before enlisting because it seemed to be recommended a lot on YouTube

Apparently, a kluge is something like a clumsy machine that nevertheless works

The idea is that our brains are bundles of these kluges, and if we can consciously recognize and correct each one in everyday life, we can keep improving beyond imagination

Apparently, the fact that humans alone among mammals get herniated discs is another evolutionary kluge

(The argument is that the advantages of walking on two legs outweigh the disadvantage of a vulnerable back)

Really makes me want to walk on all fours

It is called a self-help book, but it felt closer to a science book to me

Maybe that is because I am not yet at a level where I can fully understand it

Since so many people recommend it, I should read it again later
