Life is a succession of loves

The book's theme / Life is about continuing to seek new connections even while regretting those we missed

There was a lot I could relate to, from foolish acts of jealousy when an unrequited love starts dating someone to the process of a flirtation turning lukewarm. I liked how the chance encounter with someone new that followed felt realistic

Still, there did not seem to be anything particularly special about it
